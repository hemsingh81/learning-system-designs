## Narrative

**As** the reconciliation process
**I want** EM documents normalised to English before I try to match them against Aladdin
**So that** a Spanish trade confirmation reconciles against an English position record without an analyst translating in their head.

## Acceptance criteria summary

1. A source configured with `translate_to` has its documents passed through Azure AI Translator before the rules engine runs.
2. `broker_beta_em` is configured `language: es`, `translate_to: en`.
3. **Only descriptive fields are translated.** Field types outside `translate_field_types` are never sent to Translator. In v1 that list is `[string]`.
4. **Identifier fields are never translated regardless of type.** `account_number`, `security_id`, `isin`, `cusip`, `sedol`, `ticker`, `currency`, `trade_id` are on a no-translate list by canonical name.
5. Translation happens after field mapping, so the no-translate list is written in canonical names and does not have to be restated per counterparty in the model's vocabulary.
6. Translation does not change a field's confidence score. The score belongs to the extraction, not to the translation.
7. Direction vocabulary (`COMPRA`/`VENTA`) is normalised by a configured value map, not by translation — because "COMPRA" translated is "PURCHASE", and downstream expects `BUY`.
8. A translation failure sends the document to review. It does not proceed untranslated.

## Notes

- Criteria 3 and 4 are the fix for [NWD-138](../bug-NWD-138.md), filed by Pankaj on 2026-07-07. The original story said "translate the document to English", which is exactly the kind of sentence that reads as complete and is not. Translator obligingly turned `BBVA-MX-0041` into something else, and the match against Aladdin failed.
- Both a type allow-list and a name deny-list exist because either alone leaks. A security identifier can come back typed `string`, and a descriptive field on a new counterparty will not be on any deny-list nobody wrote yet.
