## Narrative

**As** Priya Raman, operations analyst at Northwind
**I want** any number the system was not sure about to reach me instead of the warehouse
**So that** the break report I work from contains real settlement problems and not the system's guesses, and I do not lose my afternoon proving that a break was actually a typo.

## Why this is the flagship story

Every other story in the epic moves data. This one decides whether data is allowed to move. Get it wrong in the permissive direction and the warehouse fills with numbers nobody can defend. Get it wrong in the restrictive direction and Priya reviews every document by hand, which is the process we are replacing.

The whole business case rests on one number — the straight-through rate, PRD metric M2, currently 61% against a target of 85% — and this story is the only thing that determines it.

## Acceptance criteria summary

Full Given/When/Then is in [`acceptance-criteria-NWD-103.md`](../acceptance-criteria-NWD-103.md). Summary:

1. Every extracted field is checked against a threshold chosen by its **field type**, not one global number.
2. Thresholds: currency 0.90, number 0.90, date 0.85, string 0.75, with 0.80 as the default for anything unmapped.
3. A counterparty can override a threshold. `broker_alpha` overrides currency to 0.92 because their scan quality is poor.
4. **One failing field sends the whole document to review.** No partial ingestion. See [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-one-failing-field-rejects-the-document.md).
5. A field the model did not return at all is a failure, not a pass.
6. A field returned with no confidence score at all is a failure.
7. All failures are collected and reported together — the analyst sees everything wrong in one pass.
8. A rejected document produces an exception queue row carrying field, row number, value read, confidence, threshold, and reason.
9. A passing document records `min_confidence` — the lowest confidence anywhere in it — on every row it produces.
10. The gate is pure logic with no Azure imports and no I/O, so it can be tested exhaustively without mocks.
11. **(added v1.2, after NWD-142)** A document whose declared line-item count does not match the number of line items extracted is a failure. A document where the layout model reported a table on a page that contributed no line items is a failure.

## Notes

- Ships in the same release as NWD-108 or not at all. A gate with nowhere to send rejected documents is the failure described in the handoff-contract chapter, and we are not repeating it.
- The thresholds were not chosen by intuition. Sofia and Tomas swept each field type from 0.50 to 0.99 against a labelled ground-truth set of 60 documents, took the point where auto-accepted errors on monetary fields hit zero, and moved one step up for margin. That sweep is the justification if a threshold is ever questioned.
- **v1.2, 2026-07-16.** Reopened after [NWD-142](../bug-NWD-142.md): a positions table spanning a page boundary lost five of fourteen rows and passed the gate cleanly, because every field that *was* extracted was high confidence. Criterion 11 is the fix at story level; the spec change is [`spec-confidence-gate.md` Revision 2](../spec-confidence-gate.md#revision-2--completeness-2026-07-31). The gate was answering "is this number trustworthy" and nobody had asked "is this number here".
