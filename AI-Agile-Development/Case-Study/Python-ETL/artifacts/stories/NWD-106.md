## Narrative

**As** the reconciliation process and the EM/EQ reporting modules
**I want** every counterparty document, whatever its layout and whatever its language, to arrive as rows of one shape
**So that** there is one reconciliation to write and one schema to reason about, rather than one per broker.

## Acceptance criteria summary

1. One extracted document produces zero or more `CounterpartyPosition` rows, one per line item, matching [`data-contract-counterparty-position.md`](../data-contract-counterparty-position.md) exactly.
2. Both document types land in the same shape. A position statement and a trade confirmation differ in which date columns are populated, not in structure.
3. **Money and quantity are `Decimal`, never `float`.** Quantity carries 8 decimal places, money 4. Conversion goes via `str` so the digits the model read are the digits stored.
4. Timestamps are timezone-aware UTC. A naive datetime is rejected. Northwind runs London and Los Angeles and a break report generated in one office about a row written in the other must not be arguable.
5. Every row carries `content_hash`, `bronze_path`, and `min_confidence`.
6. `min_confidence` is the lowest confidence anywhere in the document, applied to every row it produced — because a document is accepted or rejected as a unit.
7. Currency codes are ISO 4217, upper case, three characters.
8. A field mapping is configuration (`field_map`, `line_item_map`), not code. Adding a counterparty adds YAML.
9. A row that cannot be built raises `TransformError` and the document goes to review. It never produces a partial row.

## Notes

- Criterion 3 is not pedantry. The reconciliation tolerance for quantity is 0.0001. Binary floating point cannot represent 0.1 exactly, and a pipeline whose representation error is within an order of magnitude of its tolerance will produce breaks that come and go depending on which machine ran the job.
- Criterion 6 was questioned in review — why not per-row confidence? Because we do not accept documents per row. Recording a per-row confidence would imply a per-row decision that the design does not make. See [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-one-failing-field-rejects-the-document.md).
- A trade confirmation has no statement date. `statement_date` is populated from the trade date for that document type, so the natural key never contains a NULL. This is stated in the data contract and repeated here because it is the kind of detail that gets lost.
